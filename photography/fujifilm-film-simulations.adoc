=== Nostalgic Negative (X100-6 only)

The most popular custom recipe to replicate Nostalgic Negative on X-Trans IV cameras like the Fujifilm X100V is Ritchie Roesch’s "Timeless Negative" recipe from Fuji X Weekly.Because true Nostalgic Negative sits somewhere between the gentle tonal gradations of Eterna and the retro warmth of Classic Chrome, this recipe relies on the Eterna base coupled with heavy warmth shifting.🎞️ Nostalgic Negative Emulation: "Timeless Negative"Film Simulation: Eterna / CinemaDynamic Range: DR200Highlight: -1.0Shadow: -1.0Color: +4Sharpness: -1Noise Reduction: -4Clarity: -5 (Note: This can slow down saving times; use -3 or 0 if you want faster shot-to-shot performance)Grain Effect: Weak, SmallColor Chrome Effect: StrongColor Chrome FX Blue: OffWhite Balance: Auto, Shifts: Red: +3, Blue: -5
