== Nik's Silver Efex

Select from processes on the left (orange star), and the will appear on the right where you can control them.


=== Controls on the Right - Active

* Channel Mixer - effects the luminance of the colour in the image
* Film Types - like updates, including the sensitivty and tone curve
* Local adjustments & control points (use button to see mask)
Colour filter - changing hue will cycle around the colour that is affected

=== Controls on the Left - Select from
* Filters +
This can be used to, for example have the tonal response of one film type, but the grain of another film type.


=== Send back
Use "Apply"

== Filmpack
The timeline adds the filmtype pertinent to the year that you've chosen to your presets on the RH panel.
