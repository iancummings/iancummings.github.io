==== How to make an ICM Image

* moving camera

* shake reduction off!
* 1/100 for fast subject _can_ be enough
* 1/20th for staionary object _can_ be enough
* to start, try 1/2 second and go up and down
* 1/20 - 8s; 1/2 - 2s most common, sometimes 4s
* ISO lowest
* apeture high
* 2,3,6 stop ND filter
* shoot in manual or shutter priority
