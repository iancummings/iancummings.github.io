= Cooking Mushrooms

from link:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Aa3aFccue8[YouTube]

== Dos & Donts
* don't soak in water before cooking (why would you?) +
 just wash, and then dry with paper towel
* don't cut too small +
decent size mushroom should provide 4 slices
* don't add oil in at the start of the cooking +
place slices in pan, add little (10ml) water, cook over *high heat*
* don't add oil when there's still moisture in the pan (the pan won't get hot enough) +
only add oil when all moisture is gone and pan starting to show brown bits and sound has gone from simmering to sizzling; then add smallish amount of olive oil and turn down to **medium-high**.
* don't keep moving the mushrooms around they spend too little time in contact with pan +
spread them out and leave for a while (this develops deep browning and rich savoury aroma). +
once they're browned, flip them to the other side.

NOTE: we separate the moisture release stage from the browning stage


== Dos
* once browned, add a little salt
* turn heat to *medium low* and and push away to one side of pan
* add butter knob and sliced garlic to other side and let melt. +
doing this after the mushrooms have browned prevents burning butter & garlic
* once butter/garlic mix is fragrant, toss to coat all the mushrooms
* (maybe) add small amount (5g) of soy sauce to side of pan to bring out its savoury aroma
* let it sizzle briefly, then toss to coat everything
* once coated, *turn off the heat*
* serve just mushrooms, with maybe a bit of lemon and black pepper.
* the outside is browned, the insidee still juicy
