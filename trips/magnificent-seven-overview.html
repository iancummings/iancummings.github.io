"The Magnificent Seven" is a spectacular 200-mile, two-day motorcycle touring route in England. Designed by Adventure Bike Rider (ABR) magazine creators James Oxley and Billy Johnson, it was crafted to be England's answer to Scotland's famous North Coast 500. [1, 2]

The route specifically stitches together seven of the most iconic, challenging, and breathtaking biking roads across the North of England, strictly staying within the English borders so it can easily be completed over a single weekend. [1]
