== Key Stops & Waypoints Along the Route
=== Summary
If you plan to ride the route, it flows through several iconic northern landmarks and rest stops:

* Start: Bettys Café Tea Rooms (Harrogate)
* Brimham Rocks
* Coldstones Cut
* Tan Hill Inn (The highest pub in Britain)
* Alston (via Hartside Pass)
* Keswick & Buttermere
* Grasmere Gingerbread Shop
* Hardknott Roman Fort

=== Road names and nicknames
Here is the full list of the seven magnificent biking roads underpinning this route:

* FLEET MOSS (the remote one)
* BUTTERTUBS PASS (the famous one)
* CHAPEL FELL (the high one)
* HARTSIDE PASS (the long one)
* B5289 (the scenic one)
* HONISTER PASS (the lakeside one)
* WRYNOSE & HARDKNOTT PASSES (the double header)
