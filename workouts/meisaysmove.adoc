=== Shorter
Only got 10 mins to train shoulders? 

Here’s how I make every second count: link:https://www.instagram.com/reels/DUpf4L4CJoQ/[video]

* 10 slow, controlled external rotations (warm-up, see below) (3kg) +
* 8 Front-to-lateral raises  (4kg) +
* 10 Iso-hold alternating front raises  (3kg)  +
* 10 Curl-to-press  (5kg)  +
* 10 Reverse flys (3kg)  +
* 10 Alternating overhead presses (5kg)  +


1–2 min rest, then repeat for 3 rounds if you’re intermediate.

*Finisher:* Around-the-worlds to failure

Minimal kit. Maximum time-efficiency. 

You’ll hit all 3 heads of the shoulder and finish with a set of sculpted arms.
