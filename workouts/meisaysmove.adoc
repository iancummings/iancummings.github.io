=== Shorter
Only got 10 mins to train shoulders? 

Here’s how I make every second count.

✅ 10 slow, controlled external rotations (warm-up) +
✅ 8 Front-to-lateral raises +
✅ 10 Iso-hold alternating front raises +
✅ 10 Curl-to-press +
✅ 10 Reverse flys +
✅ 10 Alternating overhead presses +


1–2 min rest, then repeat for 3 rounds if you’re intermediate.

*Finisher:* Around-the-worlds to failure

Minimal kit. Maximum time-efficiency. 

You’ll hit all 3 heads of the shoulder and finish with a set of sculpted arms.
